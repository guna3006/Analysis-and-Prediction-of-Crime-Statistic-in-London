# Analysis and Prediction of Crime Statistics in London

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing historical crime data in London to uncover trends, patterns, and insights that can support public awareness and data-driven decision-making. The analysis leverages crime records from 2008 to 2016, combining statistical analysis, data visualization, geographic mapping, and predictive techniques to better understand crime behavior across the city.

## Objectives

- Analyze historical crime trends across London.
- Identify the most common crime categories and their frequency.
- Compare crime rates across boroughs and geographic areas.
- Examine the relationship between population and crime levels.
- Visualize crime distribution through charts and maps.
- Explore predictive analytics to forecast future crime trends.

## Dataset

The project utilizes publicly available crime statistics from London covering the period from **2008 to 2016**. The dataset includes information related to:

- Crime categories
- Crime counts
- Borough locations
- Monthly and yearly crime occurrences
- Crime severity classifications

## Key Analytics Performed

### Crime Trend Analysis
- Analysis of crime statistics by month and year.
- Identification of crime growth and decline patterns.
- Detection of seasonal and long-term trends.

### Crime Category Analysis
- Identification of the most frequently occurring crimes.
- Comparison between major and minor crime categories.
- Evaluation of crime severity distributions.

### Geographic Analysis
- Borough-level crime analysis across London.
- Visualization of crime density and hotspots.
- Geographic mapping of crime occurrences.

### Population vs Crime Analysis
- Comparison between borough population and crime rates.
- Exploration of correlations between population density and criminal activity.

### Predictive Analytics
- Utilization of historical crime data to explore future crime trends.
- Application of data-driven forecasting techniques to support predictive insights.
